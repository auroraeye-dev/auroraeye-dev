## 🛠 What I'm building

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🧱 [Basalt](https://basaltarch.io) — founder & sole developer**

Full-stack TypeScript/React product that renders infrastructure and industrial architecture diagrams from a text DSL. Paired with a deterministic compliance validator (IEC 62443, NIST 800-82, Purdue Model) that treats OT and ICS as first-class, not generic cloud boxes. Currently in limited pilot.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🧪 Aletheon AI — multi-agent RAG research platform**

Python, end to end: agent orchestration through to data-pipeline design. Built a 19-parameter evaluation framework and benchmarked against a leading commercial research tool — iterative fixes took one report track from **46 → 84 / 110**. Traced quality back to source-retrieval completeness and added confidence labelling with structured source anchoring.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**📊 [Silent failure modes in graph-DB benchmarking](https://github.com/auroraeye-dev)**

Reproducible benchmark of four Cypher/Bolt engines on an identical 204,109-relationship subgraph. Diagnosed four silent measurement failures that each produced credible-looking numbers — including a **285×** ingest difference traced to one missing index. Full harness and frozen dataset released.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**📡 [Samcast](https://github.com/auroraeye-dev/Samcast) — gesture-driven handoff**

Close your fist to grab a window, open your hand at another device to drop it. Swift, on-device Vision hand tracking, ScreenCaptureKit + H.264. Ports-and-adapters core with 90+ checks, no server, no accounts, no telemetry.

</td>
